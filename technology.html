1. What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a broad field focused on systems that perform tasks associated with human abilities, such as recognizing patterns, generating language, or helping make predictions. A chatbot is one kind of AI application—not the whole field.

Important: AI can sound confident and still be wrong. For important facts, compare trustworthy sources, check dates, and test instructions before relying on them.

2. Four skills worth learning first

🧭 Ask clearly

Explain your goal, current level, constraints, and the format you want back.

🔍 Verify

Ask for sources, open them, and check whether they support the claim.

🛡️ Protect privacy

Do not paste passwords, OTPs, bank details, private IDs, or confidential files into tools.

🧪 Experiment safely

Try small tasks first. Keep backups before changing important files or settings.

3. A 20-minute beginner practice

  1. Minutes 0–5: Choose one everyday question you want to understand.
  2. Minutes 5–10: Ask an AI tool to explain it in simple language with an example.
  3. Minutes 10–15: Check one important claim using a reliable source.
  4. Minutes 15–20: Write three things you learned and one question that remains.

Your goal is not to trust AI blindly. Your goal is to become better at learning, checking, and deciding.

4. A prompt you can reuse

Copy this into an AI assistant and replace the brackets with your task:

Explain [topic] to a complete beginner. Use simple language, give one real-world example, list three key points, and tell me which claims I should verify with reliable sources. Do not invent facts.
