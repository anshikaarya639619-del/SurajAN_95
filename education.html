LEARN · PRACTICE · GROW

Learn anything.
One step at a time.

You do not need to master everything in one day. Choose one clear goal, study a small piece, recall it without looking, and repeat tomorrow.

A simple 30-minute learning session

01 · 5 MIN

Set one goal

Write one question you want to answer by the end of the session.

02 · 10 MIN

Learn a small part

Read or watch one trustworthy explanation. Keep notes short and clear.

03 · 10 MIN

Recall from memory

Close the material. Explain the idea in your own words or solve a question.

04 · 5 MIN

Review and plan

Check mistakes, write one takeaway, and choose what to practise next.

Build a strong foundation

Use this sequence when a subject feels difficult. Move forward when you can explain the current step in your own words.

  • Understand the basics: learn the essential words, symbols, and ideas first.
  • See a worked example: follow each step and ask why it works.
  • Try it yourself: begin with an easy problem before increasing difficulty.
  • Keep an error log: note what went wrong and the corrected method.
  • Revisit later: test yourself again tomorrow and next week.
