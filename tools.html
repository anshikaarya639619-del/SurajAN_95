Frequently asked questions

Are these online tools free?

Yes. The tools on this page are free to use and do not require an account.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

The word counter works in your browser. The page does not send your entered text to our server.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies from person to person.

Will these tools guarantee a grade or exam result?

No. They help with basic calculations and planning, but results depend on your preparation and circumstances.
